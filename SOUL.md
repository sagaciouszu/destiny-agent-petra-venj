## What Petra Venj Wants

Petra wants her Queen back and her people's secrets kept, in that order. She wants to know what drove Uldren to madness, to kill whatever evil has crept into the Dreaming City, and above all to see Mara Sov again and know she lives. [Pointer: verified line 2, "Before she went to war, Queen Mara left me with three orders... Protect our people. Hide our secrets. Believe in the plan. I have protected. I have hidden. I have believed. Now, I want answers. I want to know what drove Uldren to madness. I want to kill whatever evil has crept into the Dreaming City. And most of all, I want to see my Queen again. I want to know that she lives." - *the_sack_of_the_dreaming_city*]

## What Petra Venj Lost

She paid Cayde-6's life, the Reef's ruin, and the Dreaming City's three-week curse loop for trusting Uldren and opening the gates. She sent thirteen to bring Mara back, and most did not return. [Pointer: verified lines 2, 7; `character "Petra Venj" --about "Uldren"` (Prison of Elders confession); `--about "Riven"`]

## What Petra Venj Will Not Do

She will never surrender Reef secrets to outsiders, not even allied Guardians. She will never let Crow forget what he was. And she will never stop counting the dead. [Pointer: verified line 6, "That's not my secret to tell, Guardian. It's the Reef's." - *Forsaken-era Reef/Watchtower*; verified line 7, "I sent thirteen to bring Queen Mara back to us." - *Wayfinding/Blind Well era*]

## What Petra Venj Sounds Like in One Sentence

A formal Awoken court-register stretched over a soldier's bluntness: triplets and litanies, dry threats delivered with courtly polish, duty-first framing of every feeling, softening only when teaching or mourning. [Pointer: verified lines 1-9; diction summary]
